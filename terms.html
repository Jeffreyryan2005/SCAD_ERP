Terms of Service & Academic Attendance Regulations

Academic Year 2026–2027 • Governing Authority: Academic Council & Controller of Examinations

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or authenticating into the SCAD Attendance ERP Portal, all students, faculty members, heads of departments, and administrative personnel agree to adhere strictly to these Terms of Service, institutional bylaws, and Anna University academic regulations.

2. Statutory Attendance Thresholds (Anna University Norms)

As per the statutory guidelines of Anna University, Chennai:

  • A candidate must secure an overall attendance percentage of not less than 75% across all enrolled theory and practical courses in a semester to be eligible to appear for the End Semester Examinations.
  • Candidates securing attendance between 65% and 74% due to validated medical reasons or sanctioned co-curricular activities may be considered for condonation subject to the approval of the Principal and payment of the prescribed condonation fee.
  • Candidates securing less than 65% overall attendance are strictly detained and must repeat the semester in the subsequent academic year.

3. Integrity & Prohibition of Proxy Attendance

The SCAD Attendance ERP is an official legal record. The following activities constitute severe academic misconduct:

  • Marking proxy attendance on behalf of another student or requesting a peer to do so.
  • Falsifying or tampering with On-Duty (OD) slips, medical certificates, or faculty reallocation approvals.
  • Sharing login credentials or security answers with unauthorized third parties.
  • Attempting unauthorized bypass of role privileges or session timeouts.

Violations will result in immediate disciplinary action by the Disciplinary Committee, including suspension of portal access and referral to the Academic Council.

4. Faculty Responsibilities

All faculty handling assigned course periods are mandated to:

  • Record student attendance within the official prescribed window (first 10 minutes of each period).
  • Document valid justification remarks whenever an On-Duty (OD) status is officially granted.
  • Verify biometric matching and cross-check physical student roster headcounts against portal entries.

5. Account Security & Access Revocation

Users are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials. Passwords must be updated periodically and meet the minimum 8-character complexity criteria. The administration reserves the right to lock or revoke credentials in the event of suspicious activity or employment/student termination.

6. Official Institutional Jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the laws of India and institutional regulations of SCAD CET. All disputes are subject exclusively to the jurisdiction of the courts of Tirunelveli District, Tamil Nadu.
