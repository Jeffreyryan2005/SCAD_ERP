Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Effective Date: Academic Year 2026–2027 • Last Revised: October 2026 • Reference: SCAD/IT/DPDP-2026/01

1. Institutional Scope & Purpose

This Privacy Policy governs the processing, retention, and protection of personal, academic, and biometric attendance records collected by the Attendance Management System (SCAD ERP) of SCAD College of Engineering & Technology (hereinafter "the Institution").

This policy complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act) 2023 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. Categories of Information Collected

To administer mandatory statutory attendance for Anna University curricular compliance, the system collects and maintains:

  • Identity Data: Student Register Number, Full Name, Department, Year, Section, Faculty Staff ID, Designation.
  • Attendance Logs: Daily period-wise attendance records (Present, Absent, Late, On-Duty), timestamps of entry, device identifiers, and faculty signatures.
  • Biometric Data: Mathematical template hashes derived from authorized biometric sensors. Raw fingerprint or facial images are never stored directly in readable formats.
  • Official Remarks: On-Duty (OD) authorization reasons, symposium/sports duty approvals, and medical leave documentation.
  • Audit & Security Logs: Authentication timestamps, IP addresses, session duration, and password change history.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

Data processing within this portal is conducted strictly for:

  • Fulfillment of statutory mandates established by Anna University and the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) regarding minimum 75% attendance criteria for semester examinations.
  • Internal academic administration, parent notification broadcasts, mentoring records, and institutional accreditation (NAAC/NBA).
  • Security monitoring and prevention of proxy or fraudulent attendance marking.

4. Access Control & Data Security

Access to student attendance profiles is strictly governed by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):

  • Students: View-only access to individual personal attendance percentages, subject breakdown, and OD status.
  • Faculty: Authorized to record and modify attendance strictly for allocated courses and assigned schedule slots.
  • Heads of Departments (HOD) & Principal: Comprehensive departmental and institutional oversight, reallocation approvals, and defaulter audits.

All data transmitted is secured using industry-standard cryptographic protocols. Passwords must adhere to institutional complexity requirements and are hashed securely.

5. Data Retention & Disposal

Attendance records are retained for the duration of the student's enrolled degree programme plus the statutory limitation period required by Anna University regulations (typically 5 academic years post-graduation). Upon expiration, academic logs are archived or expunged in accordance with SCAD IT record retention schedules.
